## The Plateau Problem: Why Scaling Breaks

Most brands hit an invisible wall when trying to scale ad spend from **$2,000/month to $20,000/month**. What worked at modest budgets suddenly collapses:
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) doubles.
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) plummets below break-even.
- Ad fatigue sets in within 5 to 7 days.

The mistake isn't the platform — it's trying to scale a micro-budget structure into an enterprise spending model.

---

## The 3-Phase Scaling Framework

### Phase 1: Creative Liquidity & Testing Sandbox ($2K - $5K)
At the early stage, never scale budget inside testing ad sets.
- Maintain a dedicated **Dynamic Creative Testing (DCT)** sandbox campaign.
- Test 3-5 visual hooks with 2 primary copy angles and 2 headline variants.
- Isolate the winning combinations with statistical significance (minimum 50 purchase events).

### Phase 2: Horizontal Scaling ($5K - $12K)
Instead of aggressively cranking up budget by 50% on a single campaign (which resets Meta's learning phase):
- Migrate verified winning creatives into a **Consolidated Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC)**.
- Deploy horizontal expansion: introduce localized demographic variants and complementary value proposition angles.
- Maintain a strict 20% budget increment cadence every 48-72 hours to prevent learning volatility.

### Phase 3: Vertical Scaling & Cost Controls ($12K - $20K+)
Once monthly spend surpasses $10K, algorithmic efficiency requires structured bid controls:
- **Bid Caps & Cost Caps**: Enforce target CPA ceilings to protect margins during high-competition auction hours.
- **Audience Layering**: Broad targeting for cold reach paired with aggressive exclusion of 30-day purchasers.
- **Server Attribution**: 100% verified CAPI event match quality ensuring Meta optimizes for true high-LTV customers.
