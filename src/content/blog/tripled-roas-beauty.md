## The Challenge

A fast-growing D2C beauty brand was struggling with diminishing returns on their Meta ad spend. Despite increasing budgets, their ROAS plateaued at 2.1x, and CPA kept creeping up month over month. iOS 14+ attribution gaps made it nearly impossible to optimize effectively — they were flying blind on which creatives and audiences actually drove revenue.

## Our Approach

### 1. Creative Overhaul with AI-Powered UGC
We replaced their polished studio creative with a systematic UGC testing framework. Using AI to identify winning hooks, angles, and formats from competitor analysis, we produced 40+ UGC variants in the first month alone. The key insight: raw, authentic content outperformed polished ads by 3.2x in hook rate.

### 2. Cloud CAPI Integration for Bulletproof Attribution
We implemented a server-side Conversions API (CAPI) layer using Stape.io + Meta's Events Manager. This recovered the 28% signal loss from browser-based pixel blocking. Every purchase, add-to-cart, and initiate-checkout event now fires from the server — no more iOS-related data gaps.

### 3. Scientific CBO Architecture
We restructured their campaigns into a 3-tier CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) framework:
- **TOF**: Broad + 1% Lookalike audiences, creative-first testing
- **MOF**: Engaged shoppers + video viewers, dynamic creative optimization
- **BOF**: Cart abandoners + high-intent visitors, advantage+ shopping campaigns

## Results in 45 Days

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| ROAS | 2.1x | **6.8x** | **+224%** |
| CPA | $42 | **$23.5** | **-44%** |
| Monthly Revenue | $87K | **$312K** | **+259%** |
| Attribution Accuracy | ~72% | **96%+** | **+24pp** |

## Key Takeaway

The combination of authentic creative at scale + server-side attribution + scientific budget allocation isn't just theory — it's a repeatable system. The brand now scales confidently, knowing every dollar is tracked and optimized.
